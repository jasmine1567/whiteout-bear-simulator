Changelog

New tools and guides, support for new generations and heroes, and usability improvements that affect you as a user.

October 8, 2026

  • Improved simulator accuracy: estimates for large marches tended to come out too high and have been revised.
  • New share image that sums up your simulator result in one picture.
  • Troop ratio now shows the current percentages and can be balanced to 100% in one tap. Presets can be added and removed freely.
  • Redesigned damage distribution chart: unlucky and lucky lines are plotted on the chart, plus a button to simulate a single rally.

October 7, 2026

  • Added hero icons. Hero faces now appear in the hero database, the Bear Hunt Simulator, generation pages and guides.
  • The simulator result now includes a chart of how likely each damage range is, with unlucky / typical / lucky figures.
  • The simulator now shows your score among players of the same generation, and generation pages add a ranking of the most-used joiner heroes (shown once enough data is in).
  • Simulator inputs are now aggregated as anonymous statistics. You can opt out anytime from the result panel; see the Privacy Policy.

October 5, 2026

  • Improved simulator accuracy: some hero skills and exclusive gear effects were revised based on user feedback.
  • Recalculated the recommended builds on the generation pages.

October 1, 2026

  • Generation 17 support: new heroes Aiden, Bertha and Eleanor added to the simulator, Left-Hero Checker, hero database and generation pages.
  • Fixed display and link issues on the English pages.

September 14, 2026

  • Redesigned the home page and menus so tools, generation builds and guides are easy to reach from any page.
  • Added an "About this tool" section (what it tells you, how to use it) to every tool page.

September 4, 2026

September 3, 2026

  • Rebuilt the generation pages as "best Bear Hunt heroes and builds": pick a spending tier to see the ideal three leaders and send the build to the simulator in one tap.
