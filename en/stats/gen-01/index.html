FAQ

What is the strongest Gen 1 rally-leader build in Bear Hunt?
Theoretical best for whales (every hero maxed): INFジェロニモG1 LANジャスミンG1 MKSジンマンG1. Mid spenders: INFジェロニモG1 LANジャスミンG1 MKSジンマンG1. F2P / light spenders: INFセルゲイG0 LANジャスミンG1 MKSジンマンG1.
Which heroes should F2P / light spenders use in Gen 1 Bear Hunt?
With no paid-only heroes and at most one non-wheel SSR (Hall of Heroes / events), the theoretical best is INFセルゲイG0 LANジャスミンG1 MKSジンマンG1. This generation’s roulette hero (F2P-obtainable) is MKS Zinman.
Should I swap to the Gen 1 heroes for Bear Hunt?
Rally-leader verdicts: INF Natalia: Strong option; INF Jeronimo: Swap in; LAN Molly: Swap in; MKS Zinman: Swap in. See “This generation’s heroes” above for the reasoning.
What if I don’t have Jeronimo for the INF slot in Gen 1?
Without paid-only heroes, INFセルゲイG0 ranks first in the INF slot.
Does the build change in Gen 2?
For whales, swapping MKS → Alonso in Gen 2 raises the theoretical value by about 51.2%. See “What to do next generation” for each tier.
How do I get the damage for my own stats instead of the theory?
Enter your formation-screen ATK% / Lethality% into the Bear Hunt Simulator. The “Simulate this build” button above opens it with this generation’s best heroes pre-set.
