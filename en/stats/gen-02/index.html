FAQ

What is the strongest Gen 2 rally-leader build in Bear Hunt?
Theoretical best for whales (every hero maxed): INFJeronimoG1 LANMollyG1 MKSAlonsoG2. Mid spenders: INFJeronimoG1 LANMollyG1 MKSAlonsoG2. F2P / light spenders: INFFlintG2 LANMollyG1 MKSAlonsoG2.
Which heroes should F2P / light spenders use in Gen 2 Bear Hunt?
With no paid-only heroes and at most one non-wheel SSR (Hall of Heroes / events), the theoretical best is INFFlintG2 LANMollyG1 MKSAlonsoG2. This generation’s roulette hero (F2P-obtainable) is INF Flint.
Should I swap to the Gen 2 heroes for Bear Hunt?
Rally-leader verdicts: INF Flint: Swap in; LAN Philly: Keep current hero; MKS Alonso: Swap in. See “This generation’s heroes” above for the reasoning.
What if I don’t have Jeronimo for the INF slot in Gen 2?
Without paid-only heroes, INFFlintG2 ranks first in the INF slot.
Does the build change in Gen 3?
For whales, swapping LAN → Mia in Gen 3 raises the theoretical value by about 27.5%. See “What to do next generation” for each tier.
How do I get the damage for my own stats instead of the theory?
Enter your formation-screen ATK% / Lethality% into the Bear Hunt Simulator. The “Simulate this build” button above opens it with this generation’s best heroes pre-set.
