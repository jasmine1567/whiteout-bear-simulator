FAQ

What is the strongest Gen 4 rally-leader build in Bear Hunt?
Theoretical best for whales (every hero maxed): INFジェロニモG1 LANミアG3 MKSアロンゾG2. Mid spenders: INFジェロニモG1 LANミアG3 MKSアロンゾG2. F2P / light spenders: INFフリントG2 LANミアG3 MKSアロンゾG2.
Which heroes should F2P / light spenders use in Gen 4 Bear Hunt?
With no paid-only heroes and at most one non-wheel SSR (Hall of Heroes / events), the theoretical best is INFフリントG2 LANミアG3 MKSアロンゾG2. This generation’s roulette hero (F2P-obtainable) is MKS Lynn.
Should I swap to the Gen 4 heroes for Bear Hunt?
Rally-leader verdicts: INF Ahmose: Strong option; LAN Reina: Strong option; MKS Lynn: Strong option. See “This generation’s heroes” above for the reasoning.
What if I don’t have Jeronimo for the INF slot in Gen 4?
Without paid-only heroes, INFフリントG2 ranks first in the INF slot.
Does the build change in Gen 5?
The theoretical best for whales does not change in Gen 5; keep your current build.
How do I get the damage for my own stats instead of the theory?
Enter your formation-screen ATK% / Lethality% into the Bear Hunt Simulator. The “Simulate this build” button above opens it with this generation’s best heroes pre-set.
