Methodology: stats & theory

Everything about how the numbers are made.

1. Live data (simulator usage)

  • Since Oct 7, 2026, what you calculate in the Bear Hunt Simulator is aggregated automatically and anonymously (before that, only form submissions were counted). Reviews are still posted through the form.
  • Press “Opt out” in the simulator’s result panel to stop sending from that device and delete what was already sent.
What we collect
  • Generation selected in the simulator, the three rally-leader heroes with gear levels, and the four joiner heroes
  • Troop tier, FC level, attack/lethality %, troop count and ratio, estimated damage
  • Estimated damage is recomputed under identical conditions: the correction factor C (standard 0.63) and advanced coefficients are reset to their defaults for the stats even if you changed them on screen. Only your own inputs differ (heroes, stats, troops, pets, buffs, trap, talent)
  • Spending tier only from people who post a review (never collected automatically)
  • Not collected: alliance, game ID, name, cookies. Only a hash of a random in-browser ID and a salted IP hash (to stop flooding) are stored
Excluding unrealistic inputs
  • Any attack/lethality % below 0 or above 3000
  • All eight stats sum to under 50 (basically empty), or all eight are the same number
  • Total troops under 1,000 or over 3,000,000
  • Data with a hero in the wrong class or not yet released in that generation is not stored
  • Excluded data stays out of all figures and is kept only for reviewing these rules
How the score is computed
  • Population: estimated damage per rally of everyone who selected the same generation (latest record per browser per generation)
  • Score = 50 + 10 × (log10 damage − mean) ÷ SD. A log scale is used because damage differs by orders of magnitude
  • Values beyond 3×IQR are removed before computing the mean and SD
  • It compares simulator estimates computed with identical coefficients, not battle records. Damage figures typed into review submissions are not used for the score
Publication rules
  • Last 90 days only
  • Fewer than 10 per generation: hidden
  • Fewer than 30: marked indicative
  • Per-tier breakdown needs 30+ overall and 10+ in the tier
Filtering
  • Damage outliers beyond 1.5×IQR removed
  • Hero/class mismatch and unreleased heroes rejected
  • Same browser and generation: only the latest record counts (one person, however many runs)
  • People who post a review are not counted twice
Bias
  • Skews toward this site’s users — engaged Bear Hunt players
  • Not a server-wide average

2. Theoretical best builds

How it is computed
  • Same formula as the Bear Hunt Simulator (assets/bear-calc.js)
  • Brute force over all obtainable INF × LAN × MKS
  • Hero expedition stats are added (unlike the simulator’s simple-input mode)
  • Skills of the same type add; different types multiply
  • Enemy DEF down (-25%) counts as x1.25 (changed Oct 5, 2026; previously about x1.14)
  • No per-hero adjustments are applied. Where the result differs from community tier lists, the computed result is shown as is
  • Index relative to each tier’s #1 (=100). No absolute damage shown
Held fixed
  • Troop ratio: 1:4:95 (heavily marksman-weighted, not shown)
  • Joiners are not fixed: for each line-up we pick the four first-expedition skills (from heroes up to that generation) that add the most damage (changed Oct 5, 2026; previously fixed to Jessie, Jasser, Seo-yoon and Wayne)
  • Base stats excluding hero expedition stats are rough guides: F2P 400% ATK / 320% Lethality, mid 700% / 580%, whale 1200% / 1050% (raised Oct 5, 2026)
  • Coefficients: the simulator’s default advanced parameters

4. Reviews (one-liners)

  • The one-liner (up to 200 chars) and optional display name (16 chars) from the submission form are shown in the generation page’s Reviews block with the build, tier and date, newest first (up to 100 per generation)
  • Posts containing links or abusive words are rejected. A review reported 3 times is hidden automatically until the operator reviews it
  • Tick “Hide my damage in the review” and the damage is used only for aggregate stats, not shown in the review
  • You can keep one submission per generation (e.g. separate reviews for Gen 10 and Gen 12). Re-submitting the same generation from the same browser overwrites (review included); “Delete my previous submission” on the form removes it. The operator may hide reviews that violate the guidelines without notice

5. Deleting a submission

  • Your edit key (saved in your browser) lets you update or delete
  • If you switched browsers, contact us with the first 6 characters of the key: Contact
