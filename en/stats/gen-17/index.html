FAQ

What is the strongest Gen 17 rally-leader build in Bear Hunt?
Theoretical best for whales (every hero maxed): INFジェロニモG1 LANミアG3 MKSエリノG17. Mid spenders: INFジェロニモG1 LANミアG3 MKSエリノG17. F2P / light spenders: INFヘクトーG5 LANミアG3 MKSエリノG17.
Which heroes should F2P / light spenders use in Gen 17 Bear Hunt?
With no paid-only heroes and at most one non-wheel SSR (Hall of Heroes / events), the theoretical best is INFヘクトーG5 LANミアG3 MKSエリノG17. This generation’s roulette hero (F2P-obtainable) is INF Aiden.
Should I swap to the Gen 17 heroes for Bear Hunt?
Rally-leader verdicts: INF Aiden: Strong option; LAN Bertha: Strong option; MKS Eleanor: Swap in. See “This generation’s heroes” above for the reasoning.
What if I don’t have Jeronimo for the INF slot in Gen 17?
Without paid-only heroes, INFヘクトーG5 ranks first in the INF slot.
How do I get the damage for my own stats instead of the theory?
Enter your formation-screen ATK% / Lethality% into the Bear Hunt Simulator. The “Simulate this build” button above opens it with this generation’s best heroes pre-set.
